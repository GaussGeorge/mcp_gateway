Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, "Session-Aware Governance for Multi-Step LLM Service Workflows," for publication in the Journal of Systems and Software.

This paper studies a software-systems problem that appears in modern agent deployments: current gateway and overload-control mechanisms usually govern individual requests, while the useful unit of work is a dependent multi-step workflow session. Under constrained or shared backends, this mismatch causes cascade waste, ambiguous recovery behavior, and replay risks for side-effecting operations.

We present PlanGate, a session-aware governance gateway for multi-step LLM service workflows. The design combines hard admission for invalid or unsafe workflow declarations, adaptive capacity-driven step-0 admission, continuation-aware ReAct governance, client-cooperative recovery, idempotent side-effect protection, and optional Redis-backed shared state for multi-gateway deployment.

The evaluation is organized by finding and scope rather than by a single benchmark. Controlled business-workflow experiments provide the main waste-reduction result. HTTP+SQLite service studies validate side-effect correctness. CloudLab experiments validate distributed checkpoint availability. Additional mock, vLLM, cloud-provider, request-level-baseline, and idempotency-only-baseline studies help interpret deployability and boundary conditions. We provide a replay validator and consolidated statistical summary package to support artifact inspection and lightweight reproducibility checks.

We believe the manuscript fits JSS because it focuses on software architecture, workflow reliability, gateway control, distributed state management, and empirical evaluation of a deployable service-system design rather than on model-quality benchmarking alone.

This manuscript is original, is not under review elsewhere, and all authors approve the submission. There are no known conflicts with JSS editorial policies.
